📌 Overview

Z Player is a modern Android media player built for people who keep their media locally.

Instead of focusing on streaming services, accounts, or cloud-dependent features, Z Player provides a clean interface for browsing, organizing, and playing your local video and audio files.

Built with Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Room, and libmpv, Z Player combines a modern UI with a powerful playback engine while keeping your media and playback data on your device.

🚀 Powered by libmpv

Z Player uses libmpv as its playback engine.

This provides Z Player with a powerful and flexible foundation for handling different media formats, codecs, subtitles, audio tracks, playback controls, and advanced playback features.

The goal is simple:

«Your media should play the way you expect it to.»

📥 Installation

1. Open the "Releases" (https://github.com/silenTKnight-sudo506/ZPlayer/releases) page.
2. Download the appropriate APK for your device.
3. Install the APK on your Android device.
4. Open Z Player and allow the required media permissions.

APK Architectures

| APK Variant | Architecture | Recommended For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

🔒 Privacy

Z Player is designed around local media and local data.

Your videos, audio files, playback history, playlists, favorites, and resume progress remain on your device unless you explicitly move or share them yourself.

Z Player does not require an online account or cloud media library to use its core functionality.
